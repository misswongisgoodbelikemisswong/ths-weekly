Numbers tell a story

入住率高，就代表賺得多？Does a full hotel mean higher profit?

ADR 是平均每日房價；入住率是已出租客房佔可供出租客房的比例；RevPAR 是每間可供出租客房的房間收入。三者均不直接量度利潤。ADR is average daily room rate; occupancy is the share of available rooms sold; RevPAR is room revenue per available room. None directly measures profit.

RevPAR = ADR × 入住率 / Occupancy
計算時將百分比化為小數，例如 80% = 0.80。Convert the percentage to a decimal: 80% = 0.80.
